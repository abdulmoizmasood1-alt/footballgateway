Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does FootballGateway guarantee a trial, contract or placement?
No. FootballGateway helps you prepare, understand your options and connect with opportunities — a trial or consultation is not a guarantee of a contract, interview or employment.
What happens if I'm not selected for a trial?
We'll talk you through the outcome and what it means for your profile, and outline realistic next steps — whether that's preparing further or exploring a different opportunity.
How does payment for the consultation work?
After you submit a request, FootballGateway contacts you by phone or WhatsApp to arrange payment and confirm your call time. Refund terms are confirmed at the time of booking.
What's included in a player trial?
It depends on your situation. Every trial starts with a profile assessment and video analysis, then we scope the rest — number of trials, accommodation, transport and flights — together with you during your consultation.
Do you work with players and coaches from any country?
Yes — FootballGateway works with players and coaches from Europe, North & South America, Australia and New Zealand looking to explore opportunities in Malta and Cyprus.
What happens after I register or book a consultation?
We review your profile and background, then get in touch to discuss your goals and the pathway that best fits your situation.
