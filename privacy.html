Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information FootballGateway collects when you use this website, and how it's used.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry, registration, trial request or consultation booking, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone / WhatsApp number (if provided)
  • Any message or details you choose to share (e.g. playing position, coaching background, goals)

We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts on this site.

How we use it

We use the information you submit only to respond to your enquiry, arrange a consultation or trial, and contact you by email, phone or WhatsApp about the service you asked about. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How it's handled

Form submissions on this site are processed by Formspree, a third-party form service, which forwards your submission to our email inbox. Formspree's own privacy practices apply to that processing step — see Formspree's Privacy Policy for details.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond to you and, where relevant, deliver the service you've booked. If you'd like your information deleted, contact us using the details below.

If you're enquiring on behalf of a minor

If the player or coach you're enquiring about is under 18, the enquiry should be submitted by a parent or legal guardian, who will be the point of contact for all further communication.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us at footballgateway00@gmail.com.

This policy is a general-purpose draft and hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. Before relying on it for a live business collecting personal data, we'd recommend having it checked against Malta/EU data protection requirements (GDPR).
