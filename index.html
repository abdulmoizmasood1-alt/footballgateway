Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does NEXORA guarantee a trial, contract or placement?
No. NEXORA helps you prepare, understand your options and connect with opportunities — a trial or consultation is not a guarantee of a contract, interview or employment.
What happens if I'm not selected for a trial?
We'll talk you through the outcome and what it means for your profile, and outline realistic next steps — whether that's preparing further or exploring a different opportunity.
How does payment for the consultation work?
After you submit a request, NEXORA contacts you by phone or WhatsApp to arrange payment and confirm your call time. Refund terms are confirmed at the time of booking.
What's the difference between the Silver, Gold and Diamond packages?
They differ in how much is included beyond the trial itself — Silver covers profile assessment, video analysis and one trial; Gold adds a second trial and accommodation; Diamond adds a third trial plus transportation and flights.
Do you work with players and coaches from any country?
Yes — NEXORA works with players and coaches from Europe, North & South America, Australia and New Zealand looking to explore opportunities in Malta and Cyprus.
What happens after I register or book a consultation?
We review your profile and background, then get in touch to discuss your goals and the pathway or package that best fits your situation.
